Cards
Each card is one roll of 1-10. 1 = Ace (1 or 11). 10 = face card. Closest to 21 without going over wins.
A round
Deal rolls 3 cards: 2 yours, 1 dealer's. You choose moves; the plugin rolls the cards. After you stand, the dealer draws to 17. 21 stands automatically.
Moves
Hit: one card. Stand: end the hand. Double: first two cards only; one card at 2x stake, then stand. Split: a pair becomes two hands, each dealt a second card.
Channels
Say radius uses /random 10. Any other choice uses /dice <channel> 10, seen only by that channel. Only your own roll in that channel counts as a card.
Round result
Off, Echo (only you see it), or posted to the roll channel as one line.
Safety
Nothing rolls until you press a button, never faster than the set spacing (2 s minimum). No result within the pause time stops rolling until you press Resume. Logging out abandons the round.
Limits
English client only. Bets and gil are not handled; stakes are units.
